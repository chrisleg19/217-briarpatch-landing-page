About the Property

A Move-In-Ready Ranch in a Quiet Cul-De-Sac

Beautifully remodeled 3 bedroom, 2 bath ranch home tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in Greenbriar. This one-level home offers 1,228 sq ft with hardwood and LVP flooring, fresh interior and exterior paint, a vaulted living area with gas fireplace, and an eat-in kitchen with quartz countertops and newer appliances.

Spacious bedrooms include a primary suite with a remodeled bath featuring a double vanity and walk-in shower. Enjoy a large fenced backyard and two-level deck, an attached garage, central air, and natural gas heat. Convenient Stockbridge location near shopping, dining, Piedmont Henry, I-75, and Hartsfield-Jackson.
